# Pinterest research - Evergreen House Thanksgiving 2026

Research date: 2026-10-17. Researcher: merchandiser. Method: signed-out Pinterest search refinement chips (pinterest.com/search/pins) for 12 queries, plus Amazon shopper-language cross-checks. Status: research only, nothing published.

## How to read this honestly

Pinterest did not show any numeric search volume to a signed-out session, and Pinterest Trends requires an ads account login, which this session did not have. So **no search volumes are claimed anywhere in this document**. What is recorded is what Pinterest itself displayed: the refinement chips (the row of suggested terms under the search bar) for each query, in the order Pinterest displayed them, plus our qualitative reading of intent and competition. Treat all of it as directional, not measured.

One incidental but important finding: the chip row on `burgundy table setting` and `dark academia thanksgiving` led with **"Less AI"**. Pinterest is surfacing an anti-AI-imagery filter as a top refinement on exactly the moody, editorial queries we intend to compete on. That is a direct instruction to us: real photography and real product shots only, no AI-looking pin art.

## Collection 1 - The Thanksgiving Table Edit

**10 keywords for this collection** (chips first, then adjacent terms we would test; all qualitative):
1. thanksgiving tablescape
2. thanksgiving table decor
3. thanksgiving table setting
4. simple elegant thanksgiving table
5. natural thanksgiving table decor
6. thanksgiving place setting
7. brown and green thanksgiving table
8. thanksgiving dinnerware set
9. thanksgiving centerpiece table
10. neutral fall table decor

**Primary intent:** "show me a table I can copy this year" - the shopper is planning, not impulse buying, and wants a complete look they can assemble. **Secondary intent:** "what dinnerware and linens do I need for a table that does not look cheap".

**Seasonal relevance:** peaks in the two weeks before November 26, with the heaviest planning window roughly October 20 to November 18. Searches keep running through the weekend after.

**Competitive visual style observed:** the mass end of this query is bright orange, burlap, pumpkin-heavy, DIY and craft-forward (note the `Diy`, `Dollar tree`, `Cheap`, `Preschool`, `Crafts for kids` chips on the centerpiece query). The elevated end is blue-and-white china, silver, and "simple elegant". Warm neutral plus brass plus cream is present as a theme (`Natural`, `Brown and green`, `Simple elegant`) but is not the dominant visual, which is our opening.

**Underserved opportunity:** a single table that is *shoppable in one sitting* at a mid price with a muted, non-orange palette. Almost every high-performing pin in this query sends to a blog post with 20 options and no direct links. A landing page where the whole table is buyable, in the actual order you set a table, is the gap.

## Collection 2 - The Moody Thanksgiving Edit

**10 keywords:**
1. moody thanksgiving table
2. burgundy table setting
3. thanksgiving tablescape (moody chip)
4. dark and moody table decor
5. burgandy thanksgiving table (deliberate misspelling, appearing as a Pinterest chip)
6. plum and brass table
7. dark academia table setting
8. vintage goblets table
9. maroon and gold table setting
10. candlelight thanksgiving table

**Primary intent:** "I want a table that looks like a magazine, not a catalog" - a specific aesthetic commitment, low volume, high loyalty. **Secondary intent:** colour coordination help (buyers search `burgundy` then `maroon and gold` then `gold and` to pair).

**Seasonal relevance:** the strongest of the four for November. Burgundy, plum and candlelight are cold-weather cues that run from mid-October through Christmas, so pins created here have a second life in December if the copy stays season-neutral.

**Competitive visual style observed:** heavy wedding contamination. The burgundy query returns `Wedding`, `Wedding rustic`, `Wedding decorations elegant`, `Maroon and gold wedding theme`, `Blush and` - meaning the dominant burgundy content on Pinterest is wedding, not Thanksgiving. Dark academia returns `Outfits` and `Wallpaper` first, so the decor intent is a minority of that phrase.

**Underserved opportunity:** Thanksgiving-specific burgundy and plum styling is thin relative to wedding burgundy. Almost nobody separates the two. A pin titled around the *table at night, lit by candles* occupies space that wedding content does not. Second gap: vintage and smoked glassware is searched but rarely sold as a set in one place.

**Primary intent:** problem-solving. The host already has a table plan and needs the *functional* gap filled: the platter big enough for a turkey, the board that fits the counter, the dispenser that does not drip. **Secondary intent:** gifting and "what to bring".

**Seasonal relevance:** high and practical, running later than the styling queries (November 1 to November 24) because it is the last-minute, need-it-now search.

**Competitive visual style observed:** this query space is dominated by recipe pins, not product pins. Ranking content is food photography with no shoppable product underneath. The product-intent minority is served by generic Amazon roundups.

**Underserved opportunity:** pairing the finished dish with the exact platter in the same image, then linking to the platter. That is a format nobody in this niche owns on Pinterest, and it converts because the shopper is already looking at the food.

**Primary intent:** curb appeal before guests arrive, with a strong "photo of my own porch" motivation. **Secondary intent:** non-tacky alternatives to orange seasonal decor.

**Seasonal relevance:** starts earliest of the four (the moment the weather turns) and keeps running into December if the greenery reads evergreen rather than pumpkin-specific.

**Competitive visual style observed:** saturated orange, cartoon and glitter pumpkins, cut-out turkeys, and "dollar store porch" content dominate. The `Less AI` chip appearing on adjacent moody queries tells us the audience is actively tired of synthetic-looking imagery, and the porch category is full of exactly that.

**Underserved opportunity:** a restrained porch in cream, olive, brass and natural timber, with zero turkeys. Almost no product pins serve it. Also underserved: layered doormat systems sold as a pair.
